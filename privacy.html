Privacy Policy

Masala Mayhem · Ikshana Solutions LLC · Last updated September 27, 2026

Masala Mayhem ("the game") is a match-3 puzzle game made by Ikshana Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play the game on iPhone or on the web at masala-mayhem.vercel.app.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. There is no sign-in.

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, boosters and sound setting) is stored only on your own device. We do not send it to our servers.

Advertising (iPhone app)

The iPhone app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, taps, videos watched)
  • Diagnostic data such as crash and performance information related to ads

This information is used by Google to serve ads, including personalized ads where permitted, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: the app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and you will see less personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you will be shown Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can choose not to consent to personalized ads.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional. The game can be played without watching them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The web version

The web version at masala-mayhem.vercel.app does not show ads. It is hosted by Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
